        Line bets, come bets, place bets, the Field and Big 6/8 must each meet the table minimum. Every payout is in whole dollars, so place bets go in $5 units ($6 on the 6 and 8, so $12 at a $10 table) and odds go in units that pay evenly. Hardways and one-roll bets start at $1.
