    Table rules

    This table

      Payouts

      • Win: 1 to 1. Blackjack: 3 to 2. Ties push.
      • A two-card 21 after a split is 21, not blackjack, and pays 1 to 1.
      • Insurance costs half your bet and pays 2 to 1 if the dealer has blackjack. Even money is the same bet when you hold blackjack.
      • Late surrender gives back half your bet. It's offered on your first two cards, after the dealer checks for blackjack.
      • Half-dollar payouts (a blackjack or surrender on an odd bet) are paid as they would be with a table's 50-cent chips.

      The dealer

      • Checks for blackjack when showing an ace or a ten. A dealer blackjack takes your bet unless you also have one.
      • Draws to 16 and stands on 17 or more, with soft 17 played as this table's rule says.
      • Plays out the hand only if you have a live hand left.

      The Hi-Lo count

      • 2 to 6 count +1, 7 to 9 count 0, tens and aces count -1, as each card is seen.
      • The true count is the running count divided by the decks left in the shoe.
      • A new shoe starts the count at 0.

      New session

      Changing the rules starts a fresh shoe. 6 to 5 blackjack adds about 1.4% to the house edge.
