    How Face Up Pai Gow pays

    The bet

    • Win both hands: paid 1 to 1, with no commission.
    • Win one hand and lose the other: push.
    • Lose both hands: you lose the bet.
    • Ties on a hand (copies) go to the dealer.
    • If the dealer's seven cards are ace-high pai gow (no pair, straight or flush, with an ace or the joker), every bet pushes.

    The joker

    • 53-card deck: the standard 52 plus one joker.
    • The joker counts as an ace, or completes a straight, a flush or a straight flush.
    • Five aces (four aces and the joker) is the best hand.
    • A-2-3-4-5 is the second-highest straight, below A-K-Q-J-10.

    High hand rankings, best first

    1. Five aces
    2. Royal flush
    3. Straight flush
    4. Four of a kind
    5. Full house
    6. Flush
    7. Straight
    8. Three of a kind
    9. Two pair
    10. One pair
    11. High card (pai gow)

    The dealer's house way (used here)

    • No pair: highest card in the high hand, next two in the low hand.
    • One pair: pair in the high hand, next two highest in the low hand.
    • Two pair: split them, unless both are 10s or lower and there's an ace to play in the low hand. Aces always split.
    • Three pair: highest pair in the low hand.
    • Trips: keep together, except three aces, which split into a pair of aces high and an ace low.
    • Straights and flushes: play them high with the best low hand left over, unless you also hold two pair.
    • Full house: pair low, trips high. Four of a kind: keep 6s and lower together, split jacks and higher, and split 7s to 10s unless there's an ace for the low hand.
