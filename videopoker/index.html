    How it plays

    Each hand

    • Bet 1 to 5 coins. Every hand comes from a freshly shuffled 52-card deck.
    • You're dealt five cards. Hold any of them, then draw: each card you don't hold is replaced from the same deck.
    • The final hand is paid from the pay table. Pays are in coins for the whole bet, so a 5-coin full house at 9 for 1 pays 45 coins.
    • The royal flush pays 250 for 1 on 1 to 4 coins and 800 for 1 (4,000 coins) on 5. The returns below assume 5 coins.
    • With 3, 5 or 10 hands, you bet the same coins on every hand. The cards you hold are copied to each hand, and each hand draws its replacements from its own freshly shuffled deck, so the hands are paid separately. The best hold is the same however many hands you play.

        Returns

        • Each return is the long-run payback with perfect play on 5 coins. Above 100% means the player has the edge.
        • The full pay tables are the best common versions. Lower pay tables cut the full house and flush (Jacks or Better) or the middle hands (Deuces Wild).
        • Payouts are always whole coins, and coins are whole dollars, so every pay is in whole dollars.
